Comeback 1.0 (1)

Personal half-marathon comeback planner for the Christie Clinic Illinois Half Marathon on April 24, 2027. Includes a dated 26-week run/walk schedule, two strength workouts, local progress tracking, JSON backup import/export and a foreground interval timer.

What to test:
- Change weeks, mark a session, add a note, close/reopen the app and confirm persistence.
- Read workout A and B and check that all exercise instructions are accessible.
- Export a progress backup, import it and verify records match.
- Import a JSON progress backup exported by the Spaces training app.
- Start, pause and reset the timer. Check sound/haptics on a physical iPhone. Leaving the app or timer screen pauses it.
- Check text at larger accessibility sizes and in light/dark appearance.

Known limits:
- No automatic cross-device or Spaces synchronization.
- No timer alerts while backgrounded or locked; timer pauses.
- The 2:10 finish is an aspiration, not a guaranteed result.

No login is required inside the app. Progress is stored locally. No analytics or advertising SDKs are included.
